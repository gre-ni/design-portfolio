Finepack is a company providing packaging solutions to both small and large companies, from paper bags to gold-foiled jewellery boxes. Their mission with this rebrand was to move closer towards a more elevated and luxury sector and to have a logomark which is more flexible and subtle, working well in practically any context and on any given material.

The core concept for this brand shift and web design was to communicate that their packaging is a vessel for stories, the keyword here being 'bespoke' solutions. From the paper bag you pick up at the local bakery to the special moment when you receive an engagement ring, bags and boxes carry different meanings in different contexts, and so does the Finepack brand.

The new logo is therefore designed to be adaptable, the 'A' symbol referencing a packaging paper fold.
