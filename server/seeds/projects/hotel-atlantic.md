Hotel Atlantic is a post-war historical fiction novel, its central theme being a long treacherous journey back home. I wanted the cover to have a soft and imaginative quality, evoking the right kind of atmosphere and themes without getting too descriptive.

There is a sense of hope and melancholy that I wished to portray, specifically by choosing the motif of a bird escaping its cage and by adjusting the colour palette to evoke the blues of the sky and sea. It is also important that the theme is not captured in a photograph but rather in a painting in order to successfully evoke its genre. The historical setting is further suggested by the choice of art deco typography for the title.
