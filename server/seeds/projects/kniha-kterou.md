This novel _(direct translation: The Book Under Scattered Star Dust)_, recounting a humorous battle of two publishers and their journey to bestseller, exists in an interesting blend of genres which of course had to be communicated through its cover. Set in the late 19th century, we wanted to achieve a steampunk feel ala Jules Verne, without it feeling overly stylised or dated.

Secondly, it was important not to veer purely into children's book territory with the illustration. It's a book which still falls into adult fiction, but it undoubtedly has a distinct whimsical narration and a touch of magical realism.

I was heavily inspired by vintage illustration styles - very decorative line work, similar to old-fashioned lithography prints. However, the intention was to also break it up with modern, flat styling for the front banner and main balloon silhouette. I believe that a fully illustrated image would be too stylised, while this balance gives the cover a unique atmosphere.
