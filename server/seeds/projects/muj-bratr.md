The book cover for Muj bratr _(in translation: My Brother)_ had a particularly difficult task: introducing a new series from an already renowned Karen Smirnoff, who is the author of the famous Girl with the Dragon Tattoo series. The challenge here was twofold:
1. This new series needed to have its own recognisable identity. With her first series being iconic, including the covers, it needed to be clear that this isn't a continuation but a completely new project.
2. Muj bratr wasn't a clear mystery or a thriller. Her story and writing could now be placed much closer to literary fiction, and so the visuals needed to reflect this switch in terms of genre.
However, even in her new literary novels, the author did not shy away from quite harrowing themes of family trauma. Communicating this in a rather subtle but specific way, I took inspiration in one of the key characters, the mother, and her preoccupation with sewing and mending.

In combination with a nostalgic vintage photograph, I created a visual metaphor for what a traumatic upbringing can feel like - piercing of a needle, again and again.
