Prazacka na vsi _(direct translation: Prague resident in a village)_ is a personal account of the experience of moving from a big metropolitan city into a small village and how hard and humorous that adjustment can be.

I chose to represent the feel of this memoir with quite a colourful yet still minimal and modern watercolour illustration. It aims to hint at the playfulness and humour of the text without steering too far into children's illustration (for this reason, it is also balanced with simple black sans-serif typography).

The second layer of meaning to this cover is the sole "empty" house: it illustrates that while all of her neighbours already have their rich, colourful histories and characters, she is there with a clean slate and is yet to build her own home in this comically foreign place.
