This publication _(direct translation: The Stories That Shall Not Be Forgotten)_ is a collection of essays where each of these essays was inspired by one interesting figure in Czech history. However, these are not necessarily famous people, but they're people who have intriguing or moving stories in their life. They either have some remarkable talent or have overcome a challenge which is quite admirable.

Therefore, my idea for this cover was to illustrate a crowd or a busy street with many anonymous people. It is to illustrate that these remarkable people could be hiding in any given crowd. The illustrations in blue are then portraying these people based on their actual photographs.
