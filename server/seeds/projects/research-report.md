Wrike's research report is an annually publlished digital document summarizing the findings of a large research project on the state of work management and productivity of knowledge workers.

For the 2024 edition, my aim was to bring a more engaging visual metaphor weaving through the entire report: the theme of a board game illustrating both the tension and collaboration between business owners and knowledge workers.

It was also one of my first experiments with including AI image generation into my workflow. I started out with a clear concept, a set of sketches and references, and then proceeded to develop a series of illustrations with the following workflow:

1. Brainstorming and sketching out compositions and collecting references for textures and overall feel for the render.
2. Creating a clear isometric composition outline (simple vector lined tiles to work from).
3. Prompting AI to use this outline alongside references and generate the board background (I used Adobe Firefly).
4. While keeping the isometric perspective, I generated the pieces and other assets separately, so that their position and rendering could be adjusted in iterative steps.
5. Lastly, I took the output of AI and polished it in Photoshop so that all of the images end up consistent and in high quality for print.

Aside from the key images, I also introduced this game metaphor throughout the rest of the infographics and layout. Key groups were assigned different "player" colours, yellow and green, so that the readers could read all graphs and diagrams with ease.
