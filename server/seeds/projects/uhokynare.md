U Hokynare is a small store in Modřany, Prague, which focuses on providing organic, good quality groceries. It is a lifestyle store as much as a provider of specialty ingredients and home goods.

In this project, I've worked with an already designed logo and helped to expand it into a brand identity: from leaflets and business cards to packaging for store's own products. One of key goals of this project was to develop brand and packaging guidelines - a helpful manual for other external designers who could be working with this brand further in the future.

The overall brand is very minimal and clean, heavily inspired by nordic minimalist design, while still rooted within the local community, leaving space for the colourful produce itself to shine.
