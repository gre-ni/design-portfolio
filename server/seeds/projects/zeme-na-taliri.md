Zeme na taliri _(direct translation: Earth on a Plate)_ is a documentary film festival about the environment, food, fast fashion, and climate. Their aim is to use documentary films, lectures, and workshops to positively and playfully seek answers to the question of how to protect our planet.

In order to provide a system that could be adopted for events in the future (making the most of the tight NGO budget), we came up with a versatile illustration system adaptable to different colour palettes and compositions.

The core idea was to reference the richness and vibrancy of various cultures featured in the educational films. While the themes can often be serious and heavy, the overall atmosphere of the event was supposed to be uplifting, warm, and inviting.
